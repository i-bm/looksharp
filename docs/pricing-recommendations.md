# Pricing Recommendations - Looksharp Subscription Packages

## Executive Summary

This document provides pricing recommendations for Looksharp's employer subscription packages based on:
- Business Requirements Document (BRD) revenue targets
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Ghana market conditions and payment preferences
- Employer personas and willingness to pay

**Recommended Pricing:**
- **Free Tier:** GHS 0/month (3 active postings)
- **Starter Tier:** GHS 200/month or GHS 2,000/year (20 active postings + branding)
- **Professional Tier:** GHS 500/month or GHS 5,000/year (Unlimited + ATS integration)

---

## Revenue Target Analysis

### BRD Targets
- **Year 1:** GHS 2.4 million with 4,000 active employers
- **Year 2:** GHS 18 million with 12,000 active employers

### Revenue Model Calculations

#### Year 1 Scenario (Conservative)
**Assumptions:**
- 4,000 total active employers
- 60% Free tier (2,400 employers) = GHS 0
- 30% Starter tier (1,200 employers) = GHS 200/month = GHS 2.88m/year
- 10% Professional tier (400 employers) = GHS 500/month = GHS 2.4m/year
- **Total Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** GHS 5.28m
- **Additional Revenue Sources:**
  - Featured postings: 20% of paid employers use 2 featured postings/month @ GHS 50 = GHS 192k/year
  - Career fair booths: 5% of paid employers participate @ GHS 1,000/year = GHS 80k/year
  - **Total Additional:** GHS 272k/year
- **Total Year 1 Revenue:** GHS 5.55m (exceeds target of GHS 2.4m)

**Note:** Conservative scenario assumes higher conversion to paid tiers. More realistic scenario below.

#### Year 1 Scenario (Realistic)
**Assumptions:**
- 4,000 total active employers
- 70% Free tier (2,800 employers) = GHS 0
- 20% Starter tier (800 employers) = GHS 200/month = GHS 1.92m/year
- 10% Professional tier (400 employers) = GHS 500/month = GHS 2.4m/year
- **Total ARR:** GHS 4.32m
- **Additional Revenue:**
  - Featured postings: 15% of paid employers use 1.5 featured postings/month @ GHS 50 = GHS 108k/year
  - Career fair booths: 3% of paid employers participate @ GHS 1,000/year = GHS 36k/year
  - **Total Additional:** GHS 144k/year
- **Total Year 1 Revenue:** GHS 4.46m (exceeds target of GHS 2.4m)

#### Year 2 Scenario
**Assumptions:**
- 12,000 total active employers
- 65% Free tier (7,800 employers) = GHS 0
- 25% Starter tier (3,000 employers) = GHS 200/month = GHS 7.2m/year
- 10% Professional tier (1,200 employers) = GHS 500/month = GHS 7.2m/year
- **Total ARR:** GHS 14.4m
- **Additional Revenue:**
  - Featured postings: 25% of paid employers use 2 featured postings/month @ GHS 50 = GHS 1.26m/year
  - Career fair booths: 8% of paid employers participate @ GHS 1,500/year = GHS 504k/year
  - **Total Additional:** GHS 1.764m/year
- **Total Year 2 Revenue:** GHS 16.164m (close to target of GHS 18m)

**Adjustment Options:**
- Increase Professional tier to GHS 600/month = +GHS 1.44m/year
- Increase featured posting usage or pricing
- Add more career fair participation
- Introduce mid-tier option (e.g., GHS 350/month for 50 postings)

## Market Research & Competitive Analysis

### Ghana Recruitment Market Context

**Market Size:**
- Ghana's formal employment sector: ~2.5 million workers
- Annual graduate output: ~50,000+ university graduates
- National Service placements: ~100,000+ annually
- Estimated recruitment market: GHS 50-100 million annually

**Key Competitors:**

1. **LinkedIn Premium (Recruiter)**
   - Pricing: ~$99-199/month (GHS 1,200-2,400/month)
   - Target: Enterprise recruiters
   - **Positioning:** Looksharp is more affordable and specialized for early-career talent

2. **Jobberman Ghana**
   - Pricing: ~GHS 300-500 per posting (pay-per-post model)
   - Target: All employers
   - **Positioning:** Looksharp offers better value with subscription model and unlimited archived postings

3. **Local Job Boards**
   - Pricing: GHS 100-300 per posting
   - Target: Small to medium businesses
   - **Positioning:** Looksharp provides better features, branding, and applicant management

4. **University Career Services (Free/Partnership)**
   - Pricing: Often free for employers
   - Target: University-specific
   - **Positioning:** Looksharp offers broader reach across all universities

### Market Positioning

**Looksharp's Competitive Advantages:**
- Specialized for early-career talent (interns, graduates, NSS)
- Mobile-first, optimized for Ghana market
- Mobile money payment integration
- Affordable pricing for small businesses
- Comprehensive applicant management tools
- University partnerships for verified talent

**Pricing Strategy:**
- **Value-based pricing:** Price based on value delivered, not just cost
- **Market penetration:** Competitive pricing to gain market share quickly
- **Tiered approach:** Multiple price points to capture different segments
- **Annual discounts:** Incentivize longer commitments

## Pricing Recommendations by Tier

### Free Tier: GHS 0/month
**Rationale:**
- Essential for market penetration and user acquisition
- Allows employers to test platform before committing
- Creates network effects (more employers = more opportunities = more talent)
- Lowers barrier to entry for small businesses
- Free tier users can become paid users as they grow

**Limitations:**
- 3 active postings limit encourages upgrades
- No branding features creates upgrade incentive
- Basic analytics encourages upgrade for insights

---

### Starter Tier: GHS 200/month or GHS 2,000/year

**Monthly Pricing: GHS 200/month**
- **Annual Equivalent:** GHS 2,400/year
- **Annual Discount:** GHS 2,000/year (17% discount, 2 months free)

**Rationale:**
- Affordable for small to medium businesses (tech startups, growing companies)
- Represents ~$25/month USD (reasonable for Ghana market)
- 20 postings sufficient for most regular recruiters
- Branding features add significant value
- Price point encourages annual commitment

**Value Proposition:**
- 20 active postings = GHS 10 per posting (vs. GHS 50-300 on competitors)
- Enhanced branding increases application quality
- Advanced analytics help optimize recruitment
- Calendar integration saves time

**Target Market:**
- Small businesses (10-50 employees)
- Growing startups
- Mid-size companies with regular hiring needs
- Companies posting 5-15 opportunities per year

**Payment Affordability:**
- GHS 200/month = ~1-2% of average small business monthly expenses
- Mobile money friendly (common transaction size)
- Annual option (GHS 2,000) = ~2 months salary for entry-level employee

---

### Professional Tier: GHS 500/month or GHS 5,000/year

**Monthly Pricing: GHS 500/month**
- **Annual Equivalent:** GHS 6,000/year
- **Annual Discount:** GHS 5,000/year (17% discount, 2 months free)

**Rationale:**
- Targets large enterprises and high-volume recruiters
- Represents ~$60/month USD (competitive with LinkedIn at fraction of cost)
- Unlimited postings essential for companies hiring 50+ interns/graduates annually
- ATS integration justifies premium pricing
- Dedicated support adds enterprise value

**Value Proposition:**
- Unlimited postings = unlimited value
- ATS integration saves significant time and costs
- Advanced analytics provide strategic insights
- Dedicated support ensures success
- ROI: One successful hire saves months of recruitment costs

**Target Market:**
- Large enterprises (MTN, Ecobank, Unilever, Nestlé)
- High-volume recruiters (100+ placements/year)
- Companies with existing ATS systems
- Organizations with dedicated HR/recruitment teams

**Payment Affordability:**
- GHS 500/month = ~0.1% of large company monthly revenue
- Annual option (GHS 5,000) = cost of 1-2 recruitment agency placements
- ROI: Platform pays for itself with 1-2 successful placements

## Additional Revenue Streams

### Featured Postings
**Pricing:**
- 7-day boost: GHS 50 per posting
- 30-day boost: GHS 150 per posting

**Rationale:**
- Affordable add-on for employers wanting extra visibility
- 7-day option for urgent postings
- 30-day option for important roles
- Can purchase multiple featured postings
- Estimated usage: 15-25% of paid employers use 1-2 featured postings/month

**Revenue Potential:**
- Year 1: 1,200 paid employers × 20% usage × 1.5 postings/month × GHS 50 = GHS 108k/year
- Year 2: 4,200 paid employers × 25% usage × 2 postings/month × GHS 50 = GHS 1.26m/year

### Sponsored Career Fair Booths
**Pricing:**
- Standard virtual career fair: GHS 500-1,000 per booth
- Premium/large career fair: GHS 1,000-2,000 per booth
- University-specific career fair: GHS 300-800 per booth

**Rationale:**
- Event-based pricing (not recurring)
- Premium placement and branding
- Access to exclusive talent pool
- Estimated participation: 3-8% of paid employers per year

**Revenue Potential:**
- Year 1: 1,200 paid employers × 3% participation × GHS 1,000 = GHS 36k/year
- Year 2: 4,200 paid employers × 8% participation × GHS 1,500 = GHS 504k/year

## Payment Method Considerations

### Mobile Money (Primary Payment Method)
**Providers:**
- MTN MoMo
- Vodafone Cash
- Telecel Cash
- AirtelTigo Money

**Transaction Fees:**
- Typical fee: 1-2% of transaction amount
- GHS 200 transaction: ~GHS 2-4 fee
- GHS 500 transaction: ~GHS 5-10 fee
- **Recommendation:** Absorb fees into pricing (already accounted for)

**Advantages:**
- High adoption in Ghana
- Instant payments
- No credit card required
- Familiar to target market

### Card Payments (Secondary)
**Providers:**
- Flutterwave
- Paystack

**Transaction Fees:**
- Typical fee: 1.5-3% + GHS 1-2 per transaction
- **Recommendation:** Pass fees to customer or absorb (decision needed)

**Advantages:**
- International cards accepted
- Recurring billing support
- Better for annual subscriptions

### USSD (Future)
**Use Case:** Feature phone users (limited market for employers)

## Pricing Psychology & Positioning

### Price Anchoring
- Professional tier (GHS 500) makes Starter (GHS 200) seem affordable
- Annual discounts create urgency and value perception
- Featured postings (GHS 50) seem affordable compared to subscription

### Value Communication
- Emphasize cost per posting (Starter: GHS 10/posting vs. competitors: GHS 50-300)
- Highlight time savings (automated workflows, ATS integration)
- Show ROI (one successful placement pays for months of subscription)
- Compare to alternatives (recruitment agencies, LinkedIn Premium)

### Payment Options
- Monthly: Lower barrier, easier to start
- Annual: Better value, predictable revenue, reduces churn
- Mobile money: Instant, familiar, no credit card needed
- Card: Professional, supports recurring billing

## Launch Pricing Strategy

### Early Adopter Incentives (First 6 Months)
- **20% launch discount** on all paid tiers
- **Starter:** GHS 160/month or GHS 1,600/year
- **Professional:** GHS 400/month or GHS 4,000/year
- Lock in pricing for first year
- Creates urgency and early adoption

### University Partnership Discounts
- **10-15% discount** for verified university partners
- Encourages university partnerships
- Increases employer trust and talent quality

### Non-Profit Organization Discounts
- **25% discount** for verified non-profits
- Supports social impact
- Aligns with platform mission

## Pricing Adjustments & Flexibility

### Monitoring Metrics
- Conversion rates (Free → Starter → Professional)
- Churn rates by tier
- Average revenue per employer (ARPE)
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Payment method preferences
- Featured posting adoption

### Adjustment Triggers
- If conversion rates too low: Consider lowering Starter tier
- If churn too high: Add more value or adjust pricing
- If revenue below target: Consider mid-tier option or increase Professional tier
- If competitors change pricing: Reassess positioning

### Future Pricing Options
- **Mid-tier option:** GHS 350/month for 50 postings (between Starter and Professional)
- **Pay-per-posting:** GHS 30-50 per posting (alternative to subscription)
- **Enterprise custom pricing:** For 50+ users or special requirements
- **Multi-country pricing:** When expanding beyond Ghana

## Implementation Considerations

### Payment Processing
- Integrate mobile money APIs (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, etc.)
- Integrate Flutterwave/Paystack for card payments
- Handle payment failures gracefully (7-day grace period)
- Support recurring billing for annual subscriptions

### Pricing Display
- Show monthly and annual options clearly
- Highlight annual savings
- Display "per posting" value for comparison
- Show feature comparison matrix

### Upgrade Incentives
- Prompt when approaching posting limits
- Show value of branding features
- Offer trial periods for higher tiers (future consideration)
- Provide upgrade discounts during launch
